# Google My Business (GMB) Setup Guide for Pet Groomers

This guide will help you set up your Google Business Profile and connect it to PetCareBooker so customers can book appointments directly from Google Search and Maps.

## Prerequisites

- A verified Google Business Profile for your pet grooming business
- A PetCareBooker account with at least one service added
- Business hours configured in your PetCareBooker dashboard

## Step 1: Claim or Verify Your Google Business Profile

### If You Don't Have a Google Business Profile Yet:

1. Go to [Google Business Profile](https://business.google.com)
2. Click "Manage now" or "Get started"
3. Enter your business name
4. Select your business category (e.g., "Pet Groomer", "Pet Service")
5. Add your business address (or service area if you're mobile)
6. Add your phone number and website URL
7. Complete the verification process (usually via postcard, phone, or email)

### If You Already Have a Profile:

1. Go to [Google Business Profile](https://business.google.com)
2. Sign in with the Google account that manages your business
3. Ensure your profile is verified (you'll see a "Verified" badge)

## Step 2: Complete Your Google Business Profile

Before enabling booking, make sure your profile is complete:

- **Business Name**: Matches your PetCareBooker business name
- **Address**: Complete and accurate (or service area for mobile groomers)
- **Phone Number**: Current and active
- **Website**: Your PetCareBooker shop URL (e.g., `https://www.petcarebooker.com/shop/your-slug`)
- **Business Hours**: Match your PetCareBooker hours
- **Photos**: Add photos of your salon, services, and happy pets
- **Description**: Include keywords like "pet grooming", "dog grooming", "cat grooming"

## Step 3: Connect Your GMB Profile to PetCareBooker

1. Log in to your PetCareBooker dashboard
2. Navigate to **Settings** → **Google My Business**
3. Click **"Connect Google Business Profile"**
4. You'll be redirected to Google to authorize PetCareBooker
5. Select the Google account that manages your business profile
6. Grant permissions for PetCareBooker to manage bookings
7. Enter your Google Business Profile ID (found in your GMB dashboard URL)

## Step 4: Enable Booking on Your GMB Profile

Once connected:

1. In your PetCareBooker dashboard, go to **Settings** → **Google My Business**
2. Toggle **"Enable Booking on Google"** to ON
3. Select which services should be bookable via Google
4. Review your booking settings (buffer time, advance booking days)
5. Click **"Save Settings"**

## Step 5: Verify Booking Appears on Google

After enabling:

1. Search for your business on Google Search or Google Maps
2. Look for the **"Book"** button on your business profile
3. Click it to test the booking flow
4. Complete a test booking to ensure everything works

**Note**: It may take 24-48 hours for the booking button to appear after enabling.

## Troubleshooting

### Booking Button Not Appearing

- **Wait 24-48 hours**: Google needs time to process the integration
- **Check verification**: Ensure your GMB profile is verified
- **Check business category**: Must be in a category that supports booking (Pet Groomer, Pet Service, etc.)
- **Contact support**: If it's been more than 48 hours, contact PetCareBooker support

### Bookings Not Syncing

- **Check connection**: Go to Settings → Google My Business and verify connection status
- **Reconnect**: Try disconnecting and reconnecting your GMB profile
- **Check hours**: Ensure business hours match between GMB and PetCareBooker
- **Check services**: Verify services are active in PetCareBooker

### Wrong Information Showing

- **Update GMB**: Make changes in your Google Business Profile
- **Update PetCareBooker**: Ensure your PetCareBooker profile matches
- **Wait for sync**: Changes can take up to 24 hours to reflect

## Best Practices

1. **Keep Information Consistent**: Ensure business name, address, phone, and hours match between GMB and PetCareBooker
2. **Respond to Reviews**: Engage with customer reviews on your GMB profile
3. **Add Photos Regularly**: Update photos to show your work and attract customers
4. **Monitor Bookings**: Check your PetCareBooker dashboard regularly for new bookings from Google
5. **Set Realistic Availability**: Only enable booking for times you can actually serve customers

## FAQ

**Q: Do I need to pay for Google Business Profile?**  
A: No, Google Business Profile is free.

**Q: Can I disable booking on Google but keep it on PetCareBooker?**  
A: Yes, you can toggle booking on/off in your dashboard settings.

**Q: Will customers see my prices on Google?**  
A: Yes, service prices will be visible when customers click "Book".

**Q: Can I customize the booking experience?**  
A: The booking flow is standardized by Google, but you control which services are bookable and your availability.

**Q: What happens if I cancel a booking made through Google?**  
A: The customer will be notified via email/SMS, just like regular bookings.

**Q: Do I need a website to use GMB booking?**  
A: No, but having your PetCareBooker shop URL as your website helps with SEO and trust.
